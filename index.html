Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

PopCalm ("the app") is a fidget and antistress toy app published by Lumio Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: PopCalm collects no data at all.

Data we collect

None. PopCalm does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party code that collects data.

Internet access

PopCalm works fully offline and makes no network requests. It does not require or use an internet connection.

Permissions

PopCalm requests no Android permissions. Haptic (vibration) feedback uses the standard system capability and involves no data.

Accounts

There is no sign-up, no login, and no account of any kind.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
